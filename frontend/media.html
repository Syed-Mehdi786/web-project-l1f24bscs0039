The Signature Roasting Process

At The Golden Bean, our coffee goes through a rigorous selection, drum-roasting, and precision brewing process. Every harvest is cupped by our head roastmaster to verify sweetness, body, and clean acidity.

Master Brewing Steps:

  1. Select high-altitude single-origin green Arabica beans.
  2. Roast in small batches at 205°C to unlock caramel aromatics.
  3. Rest beans for 48 hours and grind immediately before extraction.
  4. Brew with remineralized water at 93°C for balanced crema.
